Radical Chamber Profile Conjecture

Take two consecutive primes, like 113 and 127. Between them lies a run of composite numbers - the chamber - and each composite is a cell. Stand on the left prime p, walk k steps right, and you land on the cell p+k, provided the gap is wide enough to have that cell.

Every prime dividing your step count k is forbidden from dividing the cell you land on. If 2 divides both k = 6 and the cell p+6, then 2 must divide p itself - impossible, since p is prime. So each offset k carries a forbidden set: rad(k), the distinct primes dividing k. For k = 6 that set is {2, 3}.

The conjecture says the average divisor-count field at offset k depends on k essentially only through that forbidden set. Offsets sharing the same prime factors - 6, 12, 18, 24 all have {2, 3} - show the same profile; adding a new prime factor, like the 5 in k = 30, shifts the profile sharply, while higher powers of the same primes barely matter.

The ordering has an intuitive direction: each forbidden prime removes one of the most common divisor channels, so the more small primes are locked out, the fewer divisors the cell tends to have. The set {2, 3, 5} sits at the bottom of the ladder; the empty set {} - where every prime stays free to divide the cell - sits at the top.

The forbidden-channel lemma

The deterministic core. Everything statistical below is a probe of this constraint.

Lemma. Let p > k be prime with p+k inside the gap. No prime dividing k divides p+k.
Proof. If prime l | k and l | (p+k), then l | p; with l ≤ k < p this contradicts p prime.

Hence primes outside rad(k) are not forbidden by this lemma from dividing p+k. The lemma is PGS-native: it constrains the divisor-count field cell by cell from prime-gap structure alone.

The precise statement

On gaps wide enough to contain the probed cell, the ensemble means E(k) and T(k) cluster by radical: between-fiber separation dominates within-fiber spread by an order of magnitude, and the fiber ordering
{2,3,5} < {2,3} < {2,5} < {2,7} < {2} < {3,5} < {3,7} < {3} < {5} < {7} < ... < {}
was observed on the measured surfaces and remains conjectural outside them. Heuristic mechanism: each l in rad(k) removes the density-1/l channel of l-multiples from the divisor supply of p+k.
E(n) = (τ(n)/2 - 1) · ln n,   T(n) = τ(n)/2 - 1,   mean E(p+k) over gaps

Clustering, not identity: same-radical means differ by a real residual spread of a few field units. The live claim is the grouping and the ladder, not exact equality.

The regime is load-bearing

Only gaps with q - p > k contribute at offset k. Unrestricted mixed-length samples scramble the ladder through this selection effect. The wide-gap restriction (g > 70, k at most 64) is part of the claim, not a footnote.
